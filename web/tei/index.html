What the file contains

  • The original Perseus Latin text and editorial markup, with stable identifiers for 176 chapters and 829 sections, plus the complete Praefatio in eight sections from the edition's reader.
  • All 139 website assertions, retaining their identifiers, notes, confidence values, dates and 177 passage references.
  • Person and place registers linked to the annotations, preserving the edition's identifiers and external references.

How text and assertions are connected

Each assertion is an independent annotation linked to the chapter or chapters cited in the edition. The annotations are stored separately from the Latin text in the same file, using TEI standOff. Assertions are not nested inside other assertions.

The text has section identifiers, but the assertions currently cite chapters. Their evidence has not been assigned to individual sections. Downloading this corpus does not change the reader's chapter navigation.

Source and responsibility

The 25 Perseus texts come from PerseusDL/canonical-latinLit, with their source headers retained. The Perseus text component is distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0. The Praefatio reproduces the text of this edition's reader and is identified separately in the TEI metadata. The assertions come from the Nepos edition's dataset.
